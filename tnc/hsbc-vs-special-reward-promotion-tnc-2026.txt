HSBC Visa Signature Card 
Special Reward Promotion 
Terms and Conditions
When can you enjoy the offer
1.  The promotional period is from 1 January 2026 until 31 December 
2026, both days inclusive.
What is the offer
2.  During the promotional period, you can receive an extra 3 
times RewardCash for the Eligible Transactions made with 
your Eligible Credit Card. Your first HK$100,000 of Eligible 
Transactions are entitled for the extra RewardCash.
How can you enjoy the offer
3.  You can enjoy the offer if you:
 a.  hold an Eligible Credit Card and your credit card account is 
valid and in good standing during the promotional period/uni003B
 b.  pay with an Eligible Credit Card for any Eligible Transactions 
under the spending categories listed below during the 
promotional period:
  i) Dining –
    All Eligible Transactions at dining outlets in Hong 
Kong classified as restaurants or dining merchants 
according to the merchant codes issued by VISA 
International. Transactions made in respect of banquet 
services, private functions, private room events, dining 
outlets in hotels, department stores, associations 
and clubhouses, and the food and beverage services 
provided by the designated merchants of other 
spending categories are excluded.
  ii) Shopping –
    All Eligible Transactions made at the designated 
merchants listed in this category, excluding counters at 
department stores and outlets.
  iii) Home –
    All Eligible Transactions made at the designated 
merchants listed in this category, excluding counters at 
department stores and outlets.
  iv) Lifestyle –
    All Eligible Transactions made at the designated 
merchants listed in this category, excluding counters at 
department stores and outlets.
  v) Overseas & Mainland China Spending –
    All Eligible Transactions made overseas, in mainland 
China and Macau according to how such country/
region is coded by VISA International. Any transactions 
made in Hong Kong or in Hong Kong Dollars are 
excluded in this category.
4.  For a full list of the designated merchants within categories (ii), 
(iii) and (iv), please refer to www.hsbc.com.hk/rewards.
5.  You cannot exchange the offer for cash, other products, services 
or discounts or transfer the offer.
Read before you enjoy the offer
6.  The offer is only applicable to HSBC Visa Signature Card. For the 
avoidance of doubt, Cardholders will not be entitled to receive 
the extra RewardCash for transactions made with a private label 
card which is not linked to a personal primary or additional Visa 
Signature credit card.
7.  You have to keep all original sales slips or official payment 
records of the Eligible Transactions. In case of dispute, we may 
at any time ask you to submit these slips, records, and/or further 
evidence for inspection and we may keep them.
8.  Only the first HK$100,000 of Eligible Transactions is entitled 
to receive the offer. For calculation, Eligible Transactions 
conducted by all primary and additional credit card accounts 
under a Cardholder with the same HKID/Passport number will 
be combined/uni003B and transactions conducted in a foreign currency 
will be converted to Hong Kong Dollar. Calculation is determined 
by the transaction date of Eligible Transaction.
9.  Extra RewardCash earned with respect to each posted Eligible 
Transaction will be credited to the respective Eligible Credit 
Card account, and odd dollars spent, if any, will be carried 
forward to the next Eligible Transactions for the purpose of 
calculating RewardCash entitlement. Any odd dollars remaining 
as of a statement date will be carried forward to the subsequent 
statement months for calculating RewardCash entitlement. 
Extra RewardCash under this promotion will be credited in the 
same way as our RewardCash Programme.
10.  Each of the primary cardholder and additional cardholders under 
the same Eligible Credit Card account can enjoy the offer using 
their own Eligible Credit Cards. If the cardholder is a combined 
additional credit cardholder, both the primary and the additional 
cardholders will be able to use the extra RewardCash credited 
to the credit card account of the combined additional card.
11.  The extra RewardCash under this offer will be awarded in 
addition to the basic RewardCash under the RewardCash 
Programme that we offer.
12.  If any transaction where the extra RewardCash was awarded is 
subsequently reversed, we may deduct the extra RewardCash 
from the Eligible Credit Card account without notice to you.
13.  The terms and conditions of the Eligible Credit Card and 
RewardCash Programme will apply.
14.  We accept no liability for the quality of goods and services 
provided by designated merchants involved in this promotion.
15.  The offer under this promotion is subject to these terms and 
conditions. We can change or cancel the offer, or amend the 
list of designated merchants, or the terms and conditions. We 
will not be liable for any loss due to changes above-mentioned. 
Please check the relevant website for the latest details, 
availability and terms and conditions of the offer.
16.  If we believe that you have acted in a fraudulent or abusive way, 
you will not be able to enjoy the offer and we can deduct the 
extra RewardCash or debit your credit card to take back any 
offer you have enjoyed, or suspend/cancel your credit card.
17.  In case of dispute arising out of this promotion, our decision 
shall be final and conclusive.
18.  We write the terms and conditions of the offer under Hong Kong 
laws. In the event of any discrepancy or inconsistency between 
the English version and the Chinese version of the promotional 
materials and these terms and conditions, the English version 
shall prevail.
What these terms mean
19.  “Eligible Credit Card”  means any personal primary or 
additional HSBC Visa Signature Card issued by The Hongkong 
and Shanghai Banking Corporation Limited in Hong Kong (and 
its successors and assigns).
20.  “Eligible Transactions” are all transactions (including monthly 
purchase instalment at merchants, phone orders, mail orders 
and online purchases) made under the spending categories 
listed in Clause 3 (b), using an Eligible Credit Card during the 
Promotional Period. Eligible Transactions shall be determined at 
the sole and absolute discretion of the Bank.
  These are not Eligible Transactions:
 •  Finance and bank charges: annual fees, finance charges, 
late charges/uni003B
 •  bill payments settled via HSBC Mobile Banking and/or 
Internet Banking/uni003B
 •  purchase and/or reload of stored value cards/uni003B
 •  transactions (including top-up transactions) made via 
e-wallets/uni003B
 •  cash advance or withdrawal under the Cash Instalment 
Plan or Cash Credit Plan/uni003B monthly instalment(s) of Cash 
Instalment Plan, Cash Credit Plan, Spending Instalment 
Plan or other instalment plans (excluding monthly purchase 
instalment at merchants)/uni003B
 •  transactions at non-financial institutions (including 
purchase of foreign currency, money orders and travellers 
cheques)/uni003B
 •  transactions at financial institutions (including purchase of 
merchandise and services from banks)/uni003B
 •  wire transfers/uni003B
 •  betting and gambling transactions/uni003B
 •  tax payments/uni003B
 •  all unposted/cancelled/refunded transactions.
21.  “Basic Reward” of HSBC Visa Signature Card is $1 RewardCash 
for every HK$250 spent with Hong Kong Dollar sub-account.
